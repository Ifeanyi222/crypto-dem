Ways to grow with Crypto

Dollar-cost averaging

Auto

Buy a fixed amount on a schedule. Historically smooths out volatility but results depend entirely on the market, never guaranteed.

Long-term holding

HODL

Buy and hold through cycles. Past long-term performance of major assets has been strong, but past results don't predict future ones.

Self-directed retirement

IRA

In the US, a self-directed IRA (via a licensed custodian) can hold crypto for retirement this is not a 401(k), and rules vary by country.

Crypto markets are volatile. Nothing on this page is a guaranteed return or financial advice always do your own research and consider consulting a licensed advisor.
